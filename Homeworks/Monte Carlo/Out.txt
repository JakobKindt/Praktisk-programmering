I also investigated how the error scales here 
Uncertainties with this method has been made smaller by a factor of roughly 2 (10 times faster with built-in generator). However, now the estimated errors are generally lower than the actual errors which is probably because the estimated errors are systematic error and not the actual error of the method.
The error seemingly still follows a somewhat 1/sqrt(N) dependence but convergence faster. The 1/sqrt(N) dependence is clearer when the built-in random number generator is used.
